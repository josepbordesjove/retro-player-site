retro_player.mp3 — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

retro_player.mp3 does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • The app makes no network requests.
  • Audio files you import, the lists you make and your settings stay on your device, inside the app's own storage. They are removed when you delete the app.
  • Track information shown on the lock screen and in Control Center is handled by iOS on your device.
