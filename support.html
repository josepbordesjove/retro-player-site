retro_player.mp3 — Support

An offline dot-matrix MP3 player for iPhone.

Adding music. Tap IMPORT and pick MP3, M4A, WAV or AIFF files from the Files app, or copy them into Files › On My iPhone › retro_player.

Lists. Tap + LIST to make one. Long-press a track or a list tab for options.

Automix. The AUTOMIX bar sets the crossfade between tracks: off, 4, 8 or 12 seconds.

Display. Tap the dot-matrix display, or open SETTINGS, to change the animation.

Something not working? Email josepbordesjove@gmail.com with your iPhone model and iOS version.
